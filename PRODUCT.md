## Platform

web

## Users

- **International Specialists & Students:** Qualified foreign professionals and students (particularly in medicine, nursing, physiotherapy, engineering, and academia) seeking accredited employment, university admission, or vocational training (Ausbildung) in Germany. They face high administrative, linguistic, and regulatory barriers.
- **German Employers & Institutions:** Hospitals, clinics, elderly care homes, intensive care facilities, and educational institutions in Germany experiencing acute staffing shortages, seeking fully vetted, linguistically prepared, and credential-recognized foreign talent without handling bureaucratic burdens themselves.

## Product Purpose

ABI (Arbeit Bildung International / ABI UG) provides a dependable, end-to-end pathway connecting international talent with the German labor and educational markets. Success means enabling qualified candidates to smoothly transition into German professional life and universities with zero bureaucratic friction for either candidates or employers.

## Positioning

Full-lifecycle partnership: unlike transactional recruiters who stop at candidate introductions, ABI oversees the complete journey—from accredited domestic/overseas language preparation (A1–C1 CEFR) and official German credential recognition (Anerkennung), through visa navigation and employer placement, to on-the-ground relocation (airport reception, residency registration, bank account setup, and social insurance integration).

## Operating Context

- Operates within heavily regulated German immigration and credential frameworks (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz, Anerkennungsgesetze, Kenntnisprüfungen, Approbation / Berufserlaubnis).
- Candidates frequently navigate document apostilles, consular interviews, certified translations, and unfamiliar administrative procedures.
- German clinical and institutional HR departments require high-trust, compliant candidate vetting with clear timelines.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Language Qualification:** Tailored CEFR preparation from A1 to C1 in partnership with international language institutions.
- **Credential Recognition:** Direct representation and document handling before German recognition authorities (Anerkennungsstellen, Bezirksregierungen).
- **Placement & Matching:** Direct placement into German medical facilities, engineering firms, universities, and vocational apprenticeship programs.
- **Arrival & Onboarding:** Comprehensive arrival service (airport greeting, Einwohnermeldeamt registration, bank account opening, health insurance enrollment).
- **Constraints:** Primary language is German (`de`); must strictly adhere to German data privacy laws (DSGVO / GDPR); existing technical foundation is Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, and Tailwind CSS v4.

## Product Principles

- **Dual-Sided Trust & Clarity:** Communicate with absolute transparency and institutional seriousness to both international candidates and German healthcare directors.
- **End-to-End Continuity:** Demystify and support every phase of the relocation journey from country of origin to local German community integration.
- **Respect for Professional Dignity:** Treat candidates as valued medical and academic professionals rather than staffing commodities.
- **Uncompromising Regulatory Compliance:** Maintain rigorous adherence to German medical accreditation standards, visa requirements, and DSGVO data privacy.
